Our Heritage

Honoring Centuries of Shared Meals

Founded in Addis Ababa, Habesha Eatery brings the warmth of the Ethiopian table to life. Every morning begins with stone-ground teff fermentation and slow-simmered berbere spice blends.

We source 100% organic highland ingredients from local smallholder farms across Oromia and SNNPR to guarantee uncompromised flavor.
